Hands-On Interior Design Training

Material Study.

Material study helps students understand materials, textures, and finishes, building practical knowledge to select suitable materials for real interior design projects.

Site Vist.

Site visits help students explore real interior design projects, understand execution processes, observe materials in use, and gain practical knowledge of on-site work.

Model Makeing.

Model making helps students create design concepts, understand spatial planning, and develop practical skills by building realistic interior design models.
